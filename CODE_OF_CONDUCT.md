# Code of conduct

Niobe is built in the open by people who use it to get work done. The expectation is simple:
be the colleague you would want to review your change.

- Argue with the idea, never the person. A bug report that says what happened and what you
  expected is the most useful thing you can send.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Keep it professional: no harassment, slurs, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private
  information.
- Disagreement with a principle in `PRINCIPLES.md` is welcome in a pull request or an issue,
  with the reasoning. It is not welcome as a silent deviation.

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone
speaks for the project.

A maintainer may edit, hide or remove a comment, and may close a thread or block an account for
conduct that breaks this page. To raise a concern about conduct, use **Report a vulnerability**
under the Security tab, which is private, and say it is a conduct report.
